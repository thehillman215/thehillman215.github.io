Privacy policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Layover Supply LLC runs a small set of internal tools for its owner's personal use. One of them, a personal assistant, can read the owner's own Google Calendar events and Gmail message headers (sender, recipients, subject and date) after the owner grants access. It does not read message bodies, send email, or change anything in the account.

This data is used only to help the owner keep track of meetings and correspondence. It is stored on computers the owner controls and is never sold, shared, or used for advertising.

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Questions: owner@layoversupply.com
